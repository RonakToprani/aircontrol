## Intent

A viral-grade launch cut for AirControl, a macOS menu-bar app that turns the webcam into a touchless controller: an open hand moves the cursor, a pinch clicks and drags windows, a fist scrolls, a pinch-hold dictates, a sideways thumb switches desktops. 100% on-device — nothing recorded, nothing uploaded. The video must stop the scroll in the first second (hand → window moves), stay fast and wordless, feel like magic rather than a feature list, and end on the name + getaircontrol.vercel.app. Built for a cold social audience: X, TikTok, Reels, Product Hunt. A 9:16 cut is wanted after the 16:9 master.

## Assets

- site/index.html in the parent repo — the landing page's pure-CSS demo rig (pointer ring pinching a window) and the 21-landmark hand constellation; captured from https://getaircontrol.vercel.app
- app/Tools/AppIcon-preview.png in the parent repo — the app icon (wireframe constellation hand on a dark tile); good as the closing sting

## Customizations

- Music bed, cuts on its grid; no narration, no captions needed (text is on-screen kinetic type)
- Closing logo sting with the app icon + name + URL

## Notes

- NEVER mention a price, "free", "beta", or any pricing language anywhere in the video.
- Do not show the Vercel-looking URL more prominently than the product name; "getaircontrol.vercel.app" once, at the end, small.
- Brand: OLED-dark canvas, teal accent (#33bfd4) — the HUD ring colour — Inter.
- Hero gesture order: point → pinch-drag a window → fist scroll → thumb desktop switch → pinch-hold "speak" → close.
- "Works on your MacBook's camera. Nothing leaves your Mac." is a line worth landing.
