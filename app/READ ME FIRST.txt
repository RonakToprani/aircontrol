AirControl — control your Mac with in-air hand gestures
=========================================================

Move windows and switch desktops by waving your hand in front of the
camera. Everything runs on your Mac — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer.


INSTALL
-------
1. Drag AirControl into the Applications folder (shortcut is right here).
2. Double-click AirControl in Applications. macOS will BLOCK it the
   first time ("Apple could not verify…") — that's expected for a test
   build, not a virus warning about this app specifically.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the AirControl message. Confirm.

AirControl lives in the MENU BAR (a little hand icon, top-right of the
screen). It has no Dock icon.


FIRST RUN
---------
1. Click the hand icon → "Enable AirControl".
2. Allow CAMERA access when asked.
3. A prompt will ask for ACCESSIBILITY access (this is how it moves
   windows). Important: the dialog only OPENS System Settings — you
   must flip the AirControl switch there yourself, then come back.
4. Click the hand icon → "Calibrate hand range…" and follow the
   on-screen guide: pinch-hold at your comfortable top-left, then
   bottom-right. This makes everything feel right for YOUR arm.


GESTURES
--------
Point        Open hand, move it — the round cursor follows.
Move window  Pinch (thumb + index) on a window, move, release to drop.
Switch desktop  Make a fist, stick your THUMB out sideways pointing
             where you want to go, hold ~half a second.
Turn off     Hold a PEACE SIGN ✌ until the red meter fills.
             (Turn back on from the menu bar icon.)

Tips: sit facing the camera in decent light. If the cursor feels off,
re-run "Calibrate hand range…". The "Show hand preview" menu item shows
what the camera sees.

This is an early test build — please tell Ronak everything that felt
confusing, broken, or magical.
